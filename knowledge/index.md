## Rules

- A row points to the current page by its exact link. Never tell the AI to search for it.
- A row without an owner and a date is not a row. Delete it or fill it.
- Fixed rows change by agreement and a review. Live rows are edited in place by their owner. The AI reads a fixed file as it is and follows a live link at question time, never a copy.
- When a linked page changes, the index does not need to change. That is the point.
- When something is in neither this index nor the linked pages, the AI asks. It does not invent.
